Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "A Mass-Conservative Numerical Model of Groundwater Flow in a Three-Aquifer System with Storage in Both Aquitards, Heterogeneous Transmissivity and an Unconfined Top Layer" for consideration as an Article in *Hydrology*.

In the irrigated plains of Central Asia, pumping from deeper aquifers lowers the shallow water table through clay aquitards and thereby changes evaporation and salt accumulation in the root zone. Numerical models of such layered systems usually treat the aquitards as storage-free leakance interfaces, while analytical solutions with aquitard storage are limited to homogeneous confined layers.

The manuscript combines, in one finite-volume scheme, elastic storage in each aquitard between three aquifers, conductivity that varies in plan in all units, and a nonlinear unconfined top layer with recharge, depth-dependent evaporation and wells. The aquitard columns are eliminated exactly, so the system matrix has the size of the aquifer problem only. We prove discrete mass conservation, unconditional stability and a discrete maximum principle, and we verify the code against classical and Laplace-domain solutions, published multilayer examples, MODFLOW 6 and a field pumping test. A parametric study shows that the aquitard time scale alone does not decide when aquitard storage can be neglected; the ratio of aquitard to aquifer storage must also be considered.

The work continues two recent papers on multilayer groundwater models published in *Hydrology* (2026, 13, 181 and 13, 276) and should therefore be of interest to its readers.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. Both authors have approved the submission and declare no conflicts of interest. The code and data are openly available.
